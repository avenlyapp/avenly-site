Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 12, 2025

Avenly is a trip-planning app built with privacy as a default. Your trip data lives on your device and in your personal iCloud account — we never see it, collect it, or share it.

Information we collect

Avenly does not collect any personal information. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

All data you create in Avenly — trips, activities, lodgings, transport, notes, documents, and attachments — is stored exclusively on your device and, if you choose to enable it, in your personal iCloud account.

iCloud sync and collaboration

If you use iCloud sync or the "Plan together" collaboration feature, your trip data is stored in your private iCloud container (iCloud.app.avenly.Avenly) managed by Apple. This data is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

  • Only you and people you explicitly invite can access your trips.
  • Avenly, its developers, and any third parties cannot access your iCloud data.
  • Collaboration invites are handled entirely through Apple's CloudKit sharing infrastructure.

Data storage and security

Trip files are stored locally in your device's Documents directory as .tripplan files. If iCloud Drive is enabled, Apple encrypts this data in transit and at rest according to their security standards.

We do not operate any servers that store, process, or transmit your data.

Analytics and tracking

Avenly contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no tracking pixels, and no crash-reporting tools that send data to us. We do not track your usage of the app in any form.

Push notifications

If you enable push notifications for collaboration, Apple's Push Notification service (APNs) is used to deliver sync alerts. No notification content is sent to us — notifications are routed entirely through Apple's infrastructure.

Children's privacy

Avenly does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we collect no personal information from anyone, the app is suitable for all ages.

Your rights and data deletion

Because all data is stored on your device and in your personal iCloud account, you are in full control:

  • Delete a trip: Open the trip and tap the trash icon.
  • Delete all local data: Delete the Avenly app from your device.
  • Delete iCloud data: Go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Avenly.
  • Revoke collaborator access: Open the "Plan together" sheet and remove participants at any time.

We hold no data about you and therefore have nothing to delete or export on our end.

Changes to this policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of Avenly after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
